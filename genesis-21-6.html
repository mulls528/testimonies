Veggie Tales, Episode 29, Aired February 13, 2009

Abraham & Sarah, as told by Veggie Tales

In the episode “Abe and the Amazing Promise”, Bob the Tomato responds to a fan’s letter about waiting by directing a humorous, behind-the-scenes interview with Abraham and Sarah. The story highlights the biblical tale of their long wait for a promised son and emphasizes that God keeps His promises, even if it requires patience. The narrative is filled with comical mishaps from a distracted film crew and other characters, testing everyone’s patience along the way.
